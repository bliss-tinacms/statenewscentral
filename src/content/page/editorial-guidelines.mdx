- heading: Accuracy
  body: Every factual claim in a piece published on the site should be verifiable. Significant claims are supported by named sources, public records, or, on rare occasion, anonymous sources whose reliability has been judged by a senior editor. Errors discovered after publication are corrected on the record.
- heading: Sourcing and Attribution
  body: The publication attributes its claims. Quoted persons are identified by name and role. Studies, public records, and other reporting referenced in the coverage are credited and, where possible, linked.
- heading: Anonymous Sources
  body: Anonymous sourcing is used only when the information genuinely matters and could not be obtained any other way, and only with the knowledge of a senior editor.
- heading: News, Analysis, and Opinion
  body: News reporting is presented straight. Analysis interprets the reporting and is labeled as such. Opinion advances a position and reflects the writer rather than the publication.
- heading: Fairness
  body: Fairness means giving people and organizations covered in the reporting a meaningful opportunity to respond to material allegations before publication.
- heading: Conflicts of Interest
  body: Editors and reporters disclose financial, personal, and political relationships that could create the appearance of a conflict, and step away from coverage where appropriate.
- heading: Corrections
  body: Substantive corrections are appended to the affected article in a clearly marked corrections box, with a dated note describing what changed.
- heading: Wire and Partner Material
  body: Wire, partner, and contributor material is clearly attributed and held to the same accuracy standards as staff reporting.
