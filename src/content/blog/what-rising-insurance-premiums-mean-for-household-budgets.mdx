For many households, the insurance bill has become one of the fastest-growing line items in the monthly budget — often without any change in coverage.

## Why premiums keep climbing

Insurers point to three pressures at once: higher repair and replacement costs, more frequent severe-weather claims, and the rising price of reinsurance — the coverage insurers buy for themselves.

## How households are adjusting

Families are raising deductibles, bundling policies, shopping for new carriers, or dropping optional coverage they once considered standard.

## What states are doing

State insurance departments can review rate requests, require insurers to justify increases, and expand consumer-education programs.

## What to watch next

The next round of rate filings will show whether pressure is easing. Readers should compare deductibles, exclusions, and claim handling as well as premiums.
